Privacy Policy

Floatly for macOS · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: Floatly does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your timers, stopwatches, work log, goals, routines and settings are saved only on your own Mac. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No accounts, no tracking, no analytics

Floatly has no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code that tracks you. The app does not need an internet connection.

Away detection

To pause stopwatches when you step away, Floatly checks whether your Mac is asleep or locked and how long it has been since you last used the mouse or keyboard. It only reads that length of time. It never records what you type or click, and this information never leaves your Mac.

Purchases

The optional Floatly Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Floatly shows local notifications when a timer ends. They are created on your Mac and are not sent through any server.

Files you export

When you use "Export CSV", the file is saved only where you choose. The app cannot access other files on your Mac.

Emails

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we don't share them with anyone.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email floatly.focusapp@gmail.com.

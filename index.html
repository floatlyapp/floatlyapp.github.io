FAQ

Questions

Is Floatly free?

Yes. Timers, a stopwatch and the floating box are free forever. Pro adds the work log, routines, themes and more for €9.99, once.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep it. It also works on your other Macs signed in with the same Apple Account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Floatly never needs the internet. There's no account and nothing is uploaded.

What does away detection see?

Only how long it's been since you last touched the mouse or keyboard. Never what you type or click. Privacy policy

Will my timers show when I share my screen?

With Pro, you can hide the box from screen sharing and recordings. You still see it; your audience doesn't.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

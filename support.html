Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email floatly.focusapp@gmail.com. I usually reply within a couple of days.

For bugs, it helps to mention your macOS version and what you did just before the problem.

Common questions

Where is Floatly after I open it?

Floatly lives in the menu bar at the top right of your screen (look for the timer icon) and has no Dock icon. A small box also floats in the corner of your screen. When it's empty, it tucks itself into the menu bar until you start a timer.

How do I hide the floating box?

Hover over the box and click the hide button, or switch "Floating mini timer" off in the menu bar panel. With Pro you can also snooze it, press ⌥⌘T, or have it hide itself in full-screen apps and during screen sharing.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open the menu bar panel, choose "Upgrade to Pro" and click "Restore purchase". Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Pro works on all Macs signed in with that account.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why did my stopwatch pause?

Stopwatches pause when your Mac sleeps, locks or shuts down, so your work log only counts real time. You can turn this off in the menu bar panel. With Pro, Floatly can also ask whether to keep time you spent away from your Mac.

No sound or notification when a timer ends

Check System Settings → Notifications → Floatly and allow notifications. Also check that sounds are switched on in Floatly's menu bar panel and that your Mac isn't muted or in a Focus mode.

How do I export my work log?

In the Log tab (Pro), use the export menu to save a CSV file for a day, week, month or everything. CSV files open in Numbers, Excel and Google Sheets.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored only on your Mac, so it's included in your Time Machine backups. Deleting the app removes it.
